About The Programme

The Master of Arts in International Relations, Security, and Strategy (MA–IRSS), offered by the Jindal School of International Affairs (JSIA) at O.P. Jindal Global University (JGU), is a one-year online postgraduate programme designed to build a strong understanding of global politics, security challenges, and strategic decision-making. JGU is ranked #1 globally for Online Learning (THE, 2024 – Gold Category), ensuring a credible and high-quality learning experience.

This interdisciplinary programme equips you with analytical and strategic skills through courses in international relations, diplomacy, global governance, and conflict resolution. Taught by expert faculty, it prepares you for careers in diplomacy, policy, research, and international organisations—while offering the flexibility to learn from anywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Are scholarships available for the MA-IRSS programme?

⌄
Scholarships are not currently listed for the MA-IRSS programme. Students may check available fee payment or financing options during admission.

How are students assessed in the programme?

⌄
Students are assessed through graded assignments, quizzes, discussions, and final assessments across the programme.

What appears on the degree certificate? Is it the same as the on-campus degree?

⌄
The degree awarded is M.A. in International Relations, Security and Strategy from O.P. Jindal Global University. The degree title remains the same as the on-campus programme.

What is the eligibility criteria?

⌄
Applicants must have an undergraduate degree in any discipline from a recognised university.

What is the selection process?

⌄
Students need to submit the online application with the required documents. Shortlisted applicants are contacted by the admissions team for the next steps.​
